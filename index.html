Gleaning the Cube — pocket reference

Hold the yellow center up for the whole solve. A letter is a quarter turn of that face, clockwise as if you were looking straight at it; ' means counterclockwise, 2 a half turn. Faces: Up Down Left Right Front Back.

1 · The Daisy

Four white edge stickers around the yellow center. From the bottom (white down): half-turn its face. From the middle: turn the face its other sticker touches until it reaches the top. Top but sideways: drop it into the middle, lift it up next door.

2 · White Cross

Turn the top until a petal’s side color sits over the matching center, then half-turn that face to plant it. Repeat for all four petals.

F2 (per aligned petal)

3 · First Layer Corners

Find a white corner in the top layer, park it directly above its home slot, hold that slot at the front-right. White facing right ×1, up ×3, toward you ×5.

R U R' U'

4 · Middle Layer Edges

Pick a top edge with no yellow; match its front sticker to its center. Top sticker matches the right center → right insert; the left center → left insert.

U R U' R' U' F' U F (right)
U' L' U L U F U' F' (left)

5 · Yellow Cross

Only edges count. Line: hold it running left–right. L-shape: hug it into the back-left. Dot: line-alg, U2, then L-alg.

F R U R' U' F' (line)
F U R U' R' F' (L)

6 · Yellow Face

The Sune. Fish: lone yellow-up corner at front-left with the front-right corner’s yellow facing you. Other patterns: Sune, re-aim, Sune again.

R U R' U R U2 R'

7 · Corner Places

A corner is placed when its side colors match both neighboring faces. Hold a placed corner at the front-left as the anchor; run once or twice — it never twists corners, so the yellow face is safe. None placed? Run it once anywhere, then look again.

R' F R' B2 R F' R' B2 R2

8 · Last Edges

Hold the one solved edge at the back. The rest cycle front→right, right→left, left→front; repeat if they went the wrong way. Finish with a top-layer turn to click home.

R U' R U R U R U' R' U' R2
